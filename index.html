Choose an egg style, then start the clock as soon as the stove goes on. Tell us when the water boils for a recipe-matched finish.

Start the clock as you turn on the stove. For the first, second, fourth, and fifth choices, bring a pot of water to a boil, add the eggs, then press the button. For the cold-water method, put the egg in cold water that covers it by no more than about 1 mm; at the first bubbles, remove the pan from heat and press the button. The times follow the traditional 3-minute, longer 5–6-minute, half-boiled 7-minute, and hard-boiled 10-minute methods described in the Wikipedia article on boiled eggs. They are method-specific, not predictions of how long your stove takes to heat water; results vary with egg size, starting temperature, pot, and stove.
